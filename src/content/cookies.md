## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website or use an application. They help us recognize your device and store information about your preferences or past actions.

### Types of Cookies:
- **Session Cookies:** Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser or app
- **Persistent Cookies:** Cookies that remain on your device until they expire or you delete them
- **First-Party Cookies:** Set by us directly
- **Third-Party Cookies:** Set by third-party services we use

## 5. Your Choices and Control

### 5.1 Browser Settings
Most web browsers allow you to:
- View and delete cookies
- Block third-party cookies
- Block all cookies
- Clear cookies when closing the browser

Note: Disabling cookies may affect App functionality and your user experience.

### 5.2 Mobile Device Settings
On mobile devices, you can:
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
- **iOS (Advertising):** Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Disable "Personalized Ads"
- **Android:** Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

### 5.3 In-App Settings
You can manage certain tracking preferences within the App settings under "Privacy Preferences" or "Cookie Settings."

### 5.4 Opt-Out Tools
You can opt out of certain third-party tracking:
- Google Analytics: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
- Network Advertising Initiative: http://www.networkadvertising.org/choices/
- Digital Advertising Alliance: http://www.aboutads.info/choices/
