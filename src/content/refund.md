## 2. App Store Refund Policies

### 2.1 Apple App Store (iOS)
Apple processes all payments for iOS users. To request a refund:
- Visit: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
- Sign in with your Apple ID
- Select "Request a refund" next to the item
- Choose the reason and submit

**Apple's General Policy:**
- Refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis
- Eligible within 90 days of purchase
- Subject to Apple's Terms of Sale

For more information: https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204084

### 2.2 Google Play Store (Android)
Google processes all payments for Android users. To request a refund:
- Visit: https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory
- Find the purchase and select "Request a refund" or "Report a problem"
- Follow the on-screen instructions

**Google's General Policy:**
- Automatic refunds available within 2 hours of purchase (one-time purchases)
- Refunds within 48 hours are reviewed by Google
- After 48 hours, contact the developer (us)

For more information: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

- **Accidental Purchases:** Unintended purchases, especially by children (on a case-by-case basis)
- **Service Not Delivered:** You paid for a service/feature that was not provided
- **Misrepresentation:** The App significantly differs from what was advertised

- **Partial Subscription Period:** Unused portion of a subscription period
